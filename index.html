To request a quote for the assessment of an artwork that you are interested in buying or selling, complete this checkout form on Stripe, in which you will provide your email address and contact phone number, along with payment of the quotation fee, which will be displayed in your local currency. Within 48 hours of your payment, one of my team members will contact you to arrange submission of images of your artwork, as well as to discuss the details of the piece to provide a full quotation for the assessment of your specific artwork. Your assessment and the identity of the related artwork will be kept confidential unless you state otherwise.

Please note that the art assessment service does not guarantee that the artist intentionally included any of the hidden details I might uncover. I scan for hidden forms that the artist might have intentionally included. Nobody can be certain of an artist's true intent. Interesting forms can appear in artworks by pure chance.
