About Me

I'm a children's book author who spent almost 7 years creating my first book, Nicky's Secret: A Christmas Story, which is filled with interesting details from famous works of art.

I now offer an art discovery service for anyone looking to identify hidden details that artists might have hidden in paintings or sculptures.

Art Discovery Service & Pricing

To request a quote for the analysis of a piece of art that you are interested in buying or selling, complete this checkout form on Stripe, in which you will provide your email address and contact phone number, along with payment of the quotation fee, which will be displayed in your local currency. The link opens in a new window. Within 48 hours of your payment, one of my team members will contact you to arrange submission of images of your artwork, as well as to discuss the details of the piece to provide a full quotation for the analysis of your specific artwork. Your identity and the identity of the artwork will be kept confidential unless you state otherwise.

Please note that the art discovery service does not guarantee that the artist intentionally included any of the hidden details I might uncover. I scan for hidden forms that the artist might have intentionally included. Nobody can be certain of an artist's true intent. Interesting forms can appear in artworks by pure chance.

While any given piece of art will require a custom quotation, to give you a general idea of the fees involved, below are estimated service fees and turnaround times for artworks with various detail levels for 8-10 review passes each. Detail is not the only factor affecting pricing. For example, more expensive artworks will often require more attention coupled with a far greater number of review passes to get a reasonable certainty of catching the more overt hidden details.
